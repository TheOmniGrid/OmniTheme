# Privacy

OmniTheme is a local Discord/Vencord theme and does not add telemetry, analytics, advertising, accounts, or cloud synchronization of its own.

The theme changes presentation inside the client. Discord, Vencord, plugins, operating-system services, GitHub, Patreon, and Ko-fi remain separate products and services with their own privacy behavior.

Local theme settings and customizations stay on the user's computer unless the user deliberately shares or synchronizes them through another service.

When requesting support, redact server names, usernames, messages, account identifiers, local paths, and any other personal or third-party information from screenshots and logs.
